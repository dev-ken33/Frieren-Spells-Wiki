# Spells & Magic System

Frieren Spells features offensive magic, support magic, and civilian magic. Learn spells, develop your abilities, and unlock new ways to use offensive magic through research.

## Offensive Magic

The following offensive spells are currently implemented.

### Zoltraak

A powerful offensive spell used to attack enemies with magical projectiles.

### Juddrazilm

An offensive spell based on lightning magic.

### Reelseiden

An offensive spell based on cutting magic.

## Support Magic

Support spells provide abilities beyond direct offensive attacks.

### Defensive Magic

A defensive spell designed to protect the caster. Its activation behavior depends on the support-magic controls.

### Flight Magic

Allows the caster to use magical flight.

## Civilian Magic

### Flower Field Magic

A civilian spell that creates a field of flowers.

## Attack Patterns

Offensive magic can be used with different attack patterns. These patterns change how a spell is used in combat.

Attack patterns are unlocked through spell progression at the Research Table.

Currently planned or implemented attack pattern categories include:

* **Single Shot** — Fires a single attack.
* **Multi Shot** — Fires multiple attacks.
* **Rapid Fire** — Fires repeated attacks in quick succession.
* **Homing** — Allows attacks to track a target.
* **Special Attack** — A distinctive attack with its own behavior.

Available patterns and their effects may vary by spell and progression level.

## Learning and Progression

1. Explore the world to find spellbooks and research materials.
2. Learn spells from the spellbooks you discover.
3. Use the Research Table to increase a spell's level.
4. Unlock additional attack patterns through research.
5. Assign your learned spells through the dedicated GUI and use them in combat.

More spells and abilities may be added in future updates.
